How escalation works

Every fifteen minutes the system looks for requests past their deadline that are still unresolved.

  1. Past the deadline, it goes to the department head.
  2. Still unresolved 24 hours later, it goes to the administration.

A request the technician has already resolved is never escalated — it is waiting on the student, not the department.
